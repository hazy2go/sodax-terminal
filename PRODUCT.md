# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Crypto-native traders and DeFi power users evaluating what a full-stack cross-chain product looks like when built on the SODAX SDK. Secondary audience: builders watching the Product Hunt launch content who want proof the SDK covers real product surface area (swaps, xStocks, limit orders, lending, staking, portfolio, analytics). They arrive on a laptop, dark room or office, and judge within seconds whether this is a serious tool or another DeFi template.

## Product Purpose

SODAX Terminal is a professional cross-chain DeFi dashboard: trade (swaps, tokenized stocks, limit orders), earn (lend/borrow across 26 reserves, staking), track (cross-chain portfolio, health factor, open orders), and read the market (volume, orderbook, TVL analytics). It exists to demonstrate that one SDK can power the entire DeFi section of any neobanking app. Success: a viewer thinks "this looks like a real product I'd deposit into," and a builder thinks "I could ship this."

## Brand Personality

Sleek, modern, premium. Confident consumer-fintech polish in a pro-tool frame: the density and precision of a trading desk with the finish of a top-tier fintech app. Calm authority; numbers do the talking.

## Anti-references

- The standard DeFi template look: purple/teal gradients, glassmorphism cards, neon glow on everything.
- Bloomberg cosplay: orange-on-black retro terminal nostalgia, scanline effects, fake CRT vibes.
- Generic SaaS dashboard: identical stat-card grids, hero metrics with gradient accents.

## Design Principles

1. **Data is the interface.** Tables, prices, and rates carry the design; chrome recedes. Tabular numerals everywhere a number lives.
2. **Premium through restraint.** One accent color, exact spacing, crisp 1px borders. Expensive is quiet.
3. **Density without clutter.** Pro users want information per pixel, but every row must scan in one pass.
4. **Live by default.** Prices refresh, quotes tick, statuses stream. Motion communicates data change, never decoration.
5. **Trust at a glance.** Health factors, fees, and slippage always visible before any signature. No surprises.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast (4.5:1 body text) on the dark theme. Full keyboard navigation on tabs, tables, and forms. `prefers-reduced-motion` honored: live-data pulses become instant swaps. Color never the sole carrier of meaning (up/down also signed, statuses also labeled).
